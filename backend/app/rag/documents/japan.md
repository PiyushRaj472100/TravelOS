# Japan Travel Knowledge

## Entry Requirements

Travelers should verify current passport, visa, and entry requirements
before traveling to Japan. Requirements can depend on nationality,
purpose of travel, and length of stay.

## Transportation

Japan has extensive rail transportation. Major cities are connected
by rail, and travelers can choose between local trains, express trains,
and high-speed rail depending on their itinerary.

## Culture

Visitors should respect local customs, public behavior expectations,
and rules around photography at specific sites.

## Packing

Packing should depend on the season and regions being visited.
Travelers should check the expected weather before departure.
